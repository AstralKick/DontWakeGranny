# Launch & Ad Plan — 31 Nights at Grandma's

Rough numbers, based on how Roblox usually goes. Treat them as ballpark, not a promise.

## How ads relate to CCU
- **CCU ≈ visits per day × average session length (minutes) ÷ 1440.** With ~20-minute sessions, 1 CCU needs about 72 visits a day, so 1,000 CCU needs about 72K visits a day.
- Paid plays on Roblox (Ads Manager, Sponsored Experiences) cost roughly **$0.03–0.15 per visit**, depending on your bid, audience and thumbnail.
- So holding 1,000 CCU on ads alone would cost about **$2–10K a day**. That doesn't scale.
- **Ads are for proving your stats to the algorithm.** If retention, session length and click-through are good, Roblox's recommendations (Home, "Up Next") bring free traffic, and that's the real growth.

## Before spending anything (week -2 to -1)
1. **Final Granny rig and sounds in** (see DEVELOPER_TODO). The temporary rig won't carry thumbnails.
2. **3–4 thumbnails and 2 icons.** Big Granny face, a scared kid with a pillowcase, and a bright "31 NIGHTS" title. Roblox thumbnail A/B testing is built into the Creator Dashboard.
3. **Soft launch with friends and small Discord groups.** Fix anything that makes people quit in the first 5 minutes.
4. **Creator outreach.** Message 20–40 small-to-mid Roblox horror YouTubers and TikTokers (10K–500K subs). Offer early access and a custom in-game cosmetic. A single video from one of them can beat your whole ad budget.

## Phase 1: Test (5–7 days, ~$300)
- **$30–60 a day** on Sponsored Experiences, targeting the default horror/adventure audience, all platforms.
- Rotate thumbnails. Keep the best one, cut the rest.
- **Stats to check** (Creator Dashboard → Analytics):

| Stat | Good | Great |
|---|---|---|
| Day-1 retention | ≥ 15% | ≥ 25% |
| Day-7 retention | ≥ 5% | ≥ 10% |
| Average session | ≥ 15 min | ≥ 25 min |
| Thumbnail click-through | ≥ 4% | ≥ 7% |
| Paying users | ≥ 1% | ≥ 3% |

- **If these are weak, stop spending.** Fix the first 10 minutes (onboarding, Night 1 difficulty, the first scare) and test again. More money won't fix bad stats.

## Phase 2: Push (10–14 days, ~$1.5–4K)
- Only if phase 1 hit "Good".
- **$100–300 a day.** Raise the budget about 30% every 2–3 days while cost per visit stays under ~$0.10.
- Watch concurrent players. When Roblox starts recommending you, organic traffic overtakes paid. That's your signal to taper.

## Phase 3: Keep it alive (ongoing)
- **Every 2–3 weeks, a content drop:** a new Granny, a new map or event, a limited cosmetic.
- **At each drop, $50–150 a day for 3–5 days.** Past players get the update notification, and new players come in from the ads.
- **Seasonal events:** Halloween (Night 31 is built for it), Christmas Granny, a Valentine's event.

## Timing
**Launch 2–4 weeks before Halloween.** The game is literally about Halloween night, and Roblox horror traffic peaks in October. Save the largest ad push for about Oct 15–31.

## Budget summary

| Budget | What to expect (with good stats) |
|---|---|
| $0 (organic only) | 5–50 CCU |
| ~$300 (test only) | You learn whether the game works, then decide |
| $1.5–4K (test + push) | 200–2,000 CCU at peak, settling at 100–500 if players come back |
| + a mid-size creator video | 5K–20K+ spikes. That's where the real upside is |

## Don'ts
- Don't buy bot or "CCU boost" services. They break Roblox's rules and hurt your ranking.
- Don't run ads before the real Granny and sounds are in.
- Don't keep spending on a thumbnail below 3% click-through.
